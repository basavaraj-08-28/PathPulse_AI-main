01

📡 Real-Time Pothole Detection

Your phone's accelerometer continuously scans the road surface at 50 Hz. It detects and classifies road defects into Medium (15–25 m/s²) and High (≥25 m/s²) severity in real-time.

Accelerometer 🟡 Medium Severity 🔴 High Severity 50 Hz Sampling
02

🗺️ Live Hazard Map & Deduplication

Every detection is geotagged and plotted on an interactive map with color-coded severity pins. Smart 3-meter GPS deduplication ensures each physical pothole is stored exactly once.

GPS Geotagging Haversine Engine Color Coded
03

🚗 Pothole-Aware 3D Navigation

Navigate with turn-by-turn voice guidance. The system warns you 50 metres before known road hazards with audio alerts and visual warning banners overlaid on the 3D tilted map.

Voice Alerts 3D Camera Tilt 50m Warning
04

📊 Admin Dashboard & Export

Road authorities can inspect, edit, resolve, and export the full pothole dataset into government-ready CSV and formatted Excel reports with charts, filters, and severity analysis.
